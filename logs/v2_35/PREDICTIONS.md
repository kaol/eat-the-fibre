# v2.35 pre-registration (committed after the exploratory 1⇉2⇉3 runs, before the runs below)

Exploratory (already run, not predictions): on 1⇉2⇉3 (𝔽₅ → 𝔽₇) the local-torus growth 19(q−1) sits in 9
Aut(kQ)-invariant signature groups (module class, vanishing pattern, invariants of im P and ker P) with slopes
9, 3 (class −), 1, 1, 1 (L), 1, 1, 1 (R), 1 (LRKE), and every growing group's excess is carried by the canonical
derivations V = ⟨ad δ_aδ_b, ad δ_a, ad δ_b, E_a, E_b⟩.

Predictions:
1. 1⇉2⇇3, 𝔽₅ → 𝔽₇ (and 𝔽₇ → 𝔽₁₁): exactly 4 growing signature groups of total slope 4, three of class − and one of
   class L (Theorem 3's F1–F3 and F4).
2. 1⇉2⇇3 + 1→3 (joined ends), 𝔽₃ → 𝔽₅: no growing signature group (Conjecture 3ᶜ at the level of behaviour types).
3. On 1⇉2⇇3 the growing groups' excess is carried by ⟨ad δ_a, ad δ_b, E_a, E_b⟩ (δ_aδ_b = 0 there).

## Outcomes

1. Partly holds: total slope 4 with class split 3 (−) + 1 (L), at 𝔽₅ → 𝔽₇ and 𝔽₇ → 𝔽₁₁. But there are 2 growing
   groups, not 4: F1, F2, F3 share one signature (the invariants do not separate them).
2. Holds: 1⇉2⇇3 + 1→3 has 106 signature groups, all of the same size over 𝔽₃ and 𝔽₅.
3. Holds: the growth on 1⇉2⇇3 sits at points whose excess needs ad δ_a and ad δ_b together.
