**The correct attribution:** *Characterizing logical consequence in Paraconsistent Weak Kleene* is by **Roberto Ciuni and Massimiliano Carrara**, published in 2016 in the volume *New Developments in Logic and the Philosophy of Science* (College Publications, London, pp. 165–176) — not by Beirlaen & De Cock, and not in Studia Logica ([Studia Logica, PWK proof theory](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s11225-019-09876-z), [PWK axiomatisation and algebraic analysis](https://www.academia.edu/12860724/Characterizing_Logical_Consequence_in_Paraconsistent_Weak_Kleene)). The paper does establish exactly what I cited it for: necessary and sufficient conditions for B to be a consequence of Γ in PWK ([paper entry](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/277884122_Characterizing_Logical_Consequence_in_Paraconsistent_Weak_Kleene)); the authors also have an extended version, *Semantical Analysis of Weak Kleene Logics* ([PhilPapers](https://philpapers.org/archive/CIUSAO.pdf)).

**What happened:** the v1.2 source note attached the right result to a conflated name-and-venue — I stitched together a plausible paraconsibility-adjacent author (Beirlaen works on paraconsistent adaptive logics) with a plausible venue (the journal where the surrounding PWK literature appeared). That is precisely the kind of error the project's own corrections register exists to catch, and it took an outside check to catch it.
