# Discordianism: Open Questions Explored (v2.62): Q₀'s 366 classes in characteristic 0, and the relative criterion for the leaf

*Two results, both for Q₀ → Q₁ = Q₀ + ℓ: 2→4.
1. **Q₀'s census classes over ℚ̄, explicitly.** v2.54 proved that Q₀ has finitely many census orbits in characteristic
   0 but did not list them. Here they are listed: 366 classes with rational representatives, equal to the 𝔽_q census
   count in every one of the 21 vertex blocks.
2. **The relative criterion.** Over each of those 366 representatives P̄ the fibres of the leaf extension are computed
   exactly: the folds of Q₁ that preserve J, or preserve kQ₀, with compression P̄. On every positive-dimensional fibre
   component the stabiliser of P̄ has finitely many orbits (the v2.54 criterion, relative). So the three compressible
   kinds of v2.60 contribute finitely many census classes to Q₁. That is derived from Q₀'s classes and the fibres, not
   from Q₁'s own 90-block pipeline.

The fourth kind ("neither", 16 classes) remains.*

## 1. The 366 characteristic-0 classes of Q₀ (`analysis/v2_62_orbits.py`, `v2_62_dedupe.py`)

**Finding the orbits.** For each component Z of Q₀ and each r < dim Z, the certificate of v2.58 gives an ideal
J_{Z,r} (the prime plus the certified (r+1)-minors) with dim V(J_{Z,r}) ≤ r. An H_Z-orbit O of dimension r lies in Z_r,
so its closure is an r-dimensional component C of V(J_{Z,r}). Then O = {P ∈ C : rank [𝔥_Z, P] = r}, because in
characteristic 0 the rank is the orbit dimension.

So for each Z the procedure takes:
- a rational point of Z of rank dim Z (the open orbit);
- for each r-dimensional component C of each V(J_{Z,r}), a rational point of rank exactly r.

Of the 740 candidates, 523 have such a point, 7 more are isolated points, and 217 components showed only points of
lower rank. Those 217 are not orbit closures, on the evidence of the sampled points; that step is sampling, not proof.

**Merging into census classes.** The census class of P is generated by the census group G and the weightings
compatible at P (W(P), a linear space constant on G-orbits). Two representatives P, P′ of a block are merged when
P′ = Φ(g)φ(t)P(Φ(g)φ(t))⁻¹ has a solution over ℚ̄ with g ∈ G and t ∈ W(P) ∩ 𝔾_m^classes. This is decided exactly by a
Gröbner basis: the group parameters, inverses of the class determinants and of the weights, and the unit-ideal test.
Only pairs with equal orbit dimension and dim W(P) are tested.

**Result.** 523 representatives (plus 7 points) merge into 366 classes, and in every block the count equals the census
count at q = 3, 5, 7, 11:

| block | 000,000,000 | 000,000,001 | 001,001,001 | 100,010,001 | 100,001,001 | … all 21 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| representatives → classes | 84 → 45 | 25 → 15 | 38 → 25 | 73 → 42 | 43 → 36 | 523 → 366 |
| census | 45 | 15 | 25 | 42 | 36 | 366 |

Two qualifications:
- **Upper bound.** Merging uses single moves g·t, and census classes may chain moves, so the merged count is an upper
  bound on the number of classes over ℚ̄.
- **Completeness rests on sampling.** It depends on the 217 non-orbit-closure decisions.

The exact per-block agreement with the finite-field census supports both. It is also consistent with no 𝔽_q-class
splitting through Lang's theorem. That would hold, for instance, if the relevant stabilisers are connected; this is
not checked.

The representatives are in `logs/v2_62/q0_classes.jsonl`, each with P̄ over ℚ, W(P̄) and its orbit dimension.

## 2. The relative criterion over the 366 classes (`analysis/v2_62_relative.py`)

**Setup.** For a class representative P̄ and each shape below, every other entry of P is an unknown:
- kind J: P = [[P̄, 0], [X, P_J]];
- kind Q: P = [[P̄, Y], [0, P_J]];
- λ = 0 and λ = 1 separately.

The fold equations are built in stages, P² = P and Δ first, then closure, and presolved; the rest is decomposed in
Singular.

The census classes of Q₁ over the class of P̄, in these kinds, are the orbits of Stab(P̄) ⊆ H_{Q₁} on the fibre. Its
Lie algebra is 𝔰 = {h ∈ Der_Δ(Q₁) ⊕ E_{W_C} : [h|_{kQ₀}, P̄] = 0}. Every census derivation preserves kQ₀ and J, and
W_C is the set of weightings compatible on the fibre component C. On each positive-dimensional C the v2.54 criterion
is applied with point-guided minors: dim{P ∈ C : rank [𝔰, P] ≤ r} ≤ r for every r < dim C.

| kind, λ | empty | one point | finite (several points) | components of dim 1 / 2 / 3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| J, 0 | 0 | 173 | 193 fibres (451 points) | — |
| J, 1 | 135 | 196 | 39 points | 17 / 1 / 0, all pass |
| Q, 0 | 0 | 345 | 21 fibres (48 points) | — |
| Q, 1 | 135 | 177 | 38 points | 72 / 35 / 20, all pass |

(The 'finite' columns count fibre components of dimension 0.)

**Conclusion (char 0, given the 366 representatives).** For every class of Q₀, the folds of Q₁ over it that preserve J
or kQ₀ form finitely many census classes. λ = 0 never has moduli: every λ = 0 fibre is finite. The positive-dimensional
families occur only with λ = 1, and there Stab(P̄)° has finitely many orbits on each component, by the criterion.

**A bug found and fixed.** Four fibres (classes 83, 88, 150, 152; all kind Q, λ = 1, dim 3) first came back
unresolved. A minor with rational coefficients was printed by sympy as `…x^2/9`, which Singular reads as x^(2/9). The
minors are now written with denominators cleared, and all four pass.

## 3. Where Program 3 stands

| part of Folds(Q₁) | status (char 0) |
|---|---|
| split, λ = 0 / λ = 1 μ = 0 | trivial and identity lifts; proved (v2.60) |
| split λ = 1, and the one-sided kinds | **finitely many classes over each of Q₀'s 366 classes, by the relative criterion** (this round); the split λ = 1 part is an affine-linear bundle (v2.61) |
| neither (16 classes, 6 vertex blocks) | open: no compression; to be handled at the vertex block, where e₄ mixes with Q₀'s vertices |

**The mechanism, as far as it is now visible.** Over a class of Q, the one-sided and split lifts to Q + leaf form small
fibres:
- dimension ≤ 3 here, and finite whenever λ = 0;
- on them the stabiliser of the base fold, including the leaf's torus, acts with finitely many orbits.

What a general leaf-extension theorem would have to prove is exactly this, for any unobstructed pair:
- fibre dimension bounded by the stabiliser's rank on the λ = 1 part;
- finiteness of the λ = 0 part, together with the mixed kind.

The λ = 0 finiteness looks provable by hand, in the spirit of Corollary 4 of v2.60. That is a natural next lemma.
