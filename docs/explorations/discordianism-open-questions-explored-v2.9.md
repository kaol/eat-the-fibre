## 1. Structure theorem for folds with unital Hodge

Let H₀ ∋ 1. By v2.6 Proposition 2, conjugate the Hodge to the run-wise H = ⟨H₀, a⟩: the words whose runs all lie in H₀. Let Q₀ = q₀·k[q₀]. For the dihedral fold, q₀ = (u−b)² − s, and Q₀ is generated by q₀ because w = q₀ + s.

**Theorem 1 (proved; formula checked [N1]).**
1. **Generators.** The Podge is generated by g(f) = f − κ(f), where f runs over **generator words**: words with exactly one chaos-run, equal to q₀. Every word with chaos-runs is a product of generator leading words, after expanding Q₀-runs in powers of q₀. So ⟨g⟩ + H = A.
2. **Cofree corrections.** The coideal condition reads

   Δκ(f) = Σ_{cuts left of q₀} prefix ⊗ κ(suffix) + Σ_{cuts right of q₀} κ(prefix) ⊗ suffix.

   Its general solution is

   **κ(f) = Σ_{optional left cut, optional right cut} L₁ · a · C(m) · a · R₂,**

   where m is the middle segment containing q₀, and **C : {generator words} → H₀ is an arbitrary linear map**: the a-degree-0 part of P on generator words. This is the cofree property of the deconcatenation coalgebra, since ker Δ on H is H₀.
3. **Fold condition.** The folds are exactly the C for which P, defined by recursion on the number of a's, kills every product of generators. Each correction term has strictly fewer a's, so the recursion terminates.

For the identification fold, H₀ = k and q₀ = u. Then C(aⁱuaʲ) = C_ij, and Theorem 1 becomes v2.7's C(s, t). The engine reproduces the identification theorem of v2.8 at every number of a's: random C on chaos-first words, or on chaos-last words, gives exact folds, and mixed support fails **[N2]**.

## 2. The dihedral fold at every number of a's

**One-sided families exist but are constrained.**
- **Not arbitrary.** Random C supported on chaos-first words (q₀·a·…) is *not* a fold **[N3]**. Unlike the identification fold, Q₀ acts non-trivially on A₀/Q₀ = H₀, so v2.6's Q₀-module conditions cut the family already at first order.
- **Generic points integrate.** A generic first-order chaos-first deformation integrates to order 3 at every number of a's, with level-3 generators. Chaos-last does too, with level-2 generators and by the exact word-reversal symmetry. A generic two-sided one is obstructed at order 2 **[N5]**.

**Exact root-deviation folds (verified [N4]).** For r ∈ {b + √s, b − √s} and any μ, set

  **C(h·a·q₀) = μ·(h − h(r)),  C = 0 on every other generator word.**

This is a fold, checked exactly on all pairs of generator words up to level 6 and all triples up to level 2. The mirror C(q₀·a·h) = μ·(h − h(r)) is one as well. It fails for r = 5, for the centre r = b, and for μ₁·dev(r₊) + μ₂·dev(r₋). So these are **exact one-parameter subfamilies of C_R and C_L, one per root of the Podge's quadric**. The edge correction measures how far the Hodge run h deviates from its value at a point where the Podge vanishes.

**Retraction: the dihedral two-sided components were window artifacts.**
- **v2.6 [W9], v2.7 [K5, K6], v2.8 [L4, L5] (two-sided rows).** These used the a-degree ≤ 2 engine. Transferred into C-coordinates (the transfer is calibrated: one-sided points transfer and integrate), the two C_± points are obstructed at order 2 once all numbers of a's are included. That holds for correction bounds 5, 7 and 9.
- **The all-a-degree second-order variety [N6].** On chaos-first ⊕ chaos-last directions:
  - with level-2 generators, it has 12 two-sided solution families, 4 of them over ℚ(√2) with ratios r_±², r_±⁴, and the ℚ(√2) ones even integrate to order 4;
  - with level-3 generators, it has **none**. This persists with correction bound 5, even with some equations skipped, which can only add freedom.

**Conjecture 2.** The dihedral moduli, modulo conjugation, are **C_L ∪ C_R**. That would make every fold with unital Hodge found so far a *one-sided* deformation of the run-wise fold. The identification case is a theorem (v2.8).

*Methodological note.* In this round, every enlargement of the window (more a's, larger levels, larger correction bounds) removed structure and never added any. The run of results v2.6 → v2.8 on C_± is a cautionary example. Each finite truncation produced a clean, Galois-symmetric, integrable-looking component, and each died in the next truncation.

**Gloss.** *Chaos may lean on an event from the left or from the right. It may lean exactly, from one side, by measuring how far the order strays from a point where chaos itself vanishes. Leaning from both sides at once only ever looked possible through a keyhole.*

## 4. New open questions

1. **Prove Conjecture 2.** Lift v2.8's functional-equation proof, which uses exponentials in a, to C-coordinates with H₀-valued contexts. The two-generator consistency should again force one-sided support.
2. **The full one-sided family.** Root deviations are 1-dimensional per root. The first-order one-sided tangent has 5 dimensions at level ≤ 2. What is the complete description: a cyclic-module form analogous to ρ(u) = F(D)?
3. **Why roots?** Find a conceptual reason that μ(h − h(r)) needs (u−b)² − s to vanish at r. One candidate: the Podge embeds in the identification Podge J_r = ker(u ↦ r).
4. **Framed folds** (non-unital Hodge) in a C-type framework.
5. Carried: the counting sequence (a(10) = 25665390); quiver moduli; Borelness.
