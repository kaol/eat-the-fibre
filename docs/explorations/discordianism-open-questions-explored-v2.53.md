# Discordianism: Open Questions Explored (v2.53): the fold variety of 1⇉2⇇3 + 1→3 has 118 components, each with an open census orbit

*v2.52 showed that on Q₀ = 1⇉2⇇3 + 1→3 the vertex block of a fold takes 21 values. This round splits the folds into
the 21 vertex-block strata and decomposes each over ℚ. In total there are 118 irreducible components. Every component
of maximal dimension in its stratum is absolutely irreducible. Every one of the 118 components contains a dense orbit
of the connected census group: Δ-compatible automorphisms, times the torus of the class weightings compatible on the
component. So there is no generic modulus anywhere on Q₀. What remains for lemma (L) are the boundaries: the
complement of each open orbit in its component. All four pre-registered predictions held in substance; 50 failed as
stated because of a crude estimator.*

## 1. The theorem

Let k be a field of characteristic 0 and Q₀ = 1⇉2⇇3 + 1→3.

**Theorem.**
1. Folds(kQ₀) is the disjoint union of the 21 strata S_V (v2.52). Each S_V is open and closed, since V takes finitely
   many values.
2. Over ℚ the strata have 118 irreducible components in total:

   | dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | components | 7 | 15 | 23 | 39 | 19 | 13 | 2 |

   The 40 that are of maximal dimension in their stratum are absolutely irreducible.
3. For each component Z, let H_Z be the connected group generated by the Δ-compatible automorphisms and the torus of
   class weightings w compatible with every fold of Z. Then H_Z has a dense orbit in Z.

**Proof.**
- **Step 1.** v2.52.
- **Step 2.** Each stratum is parametrised by P_K, with the vertex block fixed, and by P on a complement of K: 66
  unknowns, with all fold equations. Linear equations with constant coefficients are solved away exactly, which leaves
  between 5 and 30 unknowns. Then `minAssGTZ` over ℚ gives the components, and `absPrimdecGTZ` checks the components
  of maximal dimension (`analysis/v2_53_stratum.py`).
- **Step 3** (`analysis/v2_53_orbits.py`):
  - W_Z is computed exactly. The class defects of P are reduced modulo a Gröbner basis of the component's prime, and
    W_Z is the kernel of w ↦ Σ_c w_c NF(defect_c).
  - H_Z preserves Z. It maps folds with W ⊆ W_P to folds, and it fixes vertices, so it preserves the stratum and its
    components (H_Z is connected).
  - At a rational point P of Z, found by fixing a maximal independent set at random integers and solving the rest,
    rank [Lie H_Z, P] = dim Z. The rank at one point is a lower bound for the generic rank, so the orbit of P has the
    dimension of Z. Z is irreducible, so that orbit is dense, hence open. ∎

The weightings that occur: on 92 components all three class weights are free. On 17 components W_Z = {w_a = w_c}, and
on 9 components W_Z = {w_a = w_b}. Classes are ordered a, b, c, where c is the single arrow 1→3.

## 2. What this gives for Conjecture 3ᶜ on Q₀

- **No generic modulus.** On each component a single census orbit is dense, so the census orbits that meet the
  generic points of components are exactly 118 over k̄. A one-parameter family of census orbits, the mechanism of
  Theorems 3 and 4, could only sit inside the complements Z \ O_Z, which have dimension less than dim Z.
- **The count is consistent.** There are 366 local-torus orbits at every prime tested. 118 of them are the open orbits
  (over 𝔽_q, possibly split by finite stabiliser component groups). The other 248 must then lie in the boundaries.
  This is the next computation.
- **What is missing for a full proof of (L) on Q₀.** The boundary recursion: for each Z, the closed set
  {P ∈ Z : rank [𝔥_Z, P] < dim Z} = Z \ O_Z. Compute its components, as with the divisor in v2.47–v2.48. On each such
  component, find an open orbit of the group of the weightings compatible there, and recurse down to dimension 0. If
  every step succeeds, Folds(k̄Q₀) is a finite union of census-group orbits. That is lemma (L) in a strong form for Q₀,
  and with a uniformity argument it would give Conjecture 3ᶜ for this quiver.
- **Contrast.** On the unjoined 1⇉2⇇3 the analogue of item 3 must fail. The census group fixes the vertex block,
  which is continuous on a component. An open orbit would make the block constant on a dense set, hence on the whole
  component. But Theorem 3's families vary the block inside a component.
