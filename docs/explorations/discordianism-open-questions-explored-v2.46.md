# Discordianism: Open Questions Explored (v2.46): a dominant slice for the fixed-image variety

*Following a ChatGPT brief (docs/briefs/chatgpt-v2.45-frontier.md) with three tasks. Task 3, the dominant-slice

## 0. The theorem

**Theorem (characteristic 0).**
- Setting: 1⇉2⇉3 + 1→3 over an algebraically closed field of characteristic 0. P₀ is the integer fold rep 5107
  (v2.44), N = u·wᵀ, and P_t = P₀ + t·N.
- A = im P₀, and V_A is the variety of folds with image A.

Then:
1. **V_A is irreducible of dimension 7.**
2. **The census symmetries fix a dominant slice.**
   - Let H′ be the group generated by the Δ-compatible automorphisms and the class weightings λ = (s, r, s), and
     let H′_A be its subgroup preserving A.
   - Then Φ : H′_A × 𝔸¹ → V_A, (h, t) ↦ h·P_t·h⁻¹, is a dominant morphism.
3. **Each behaviour meets the line finitely.** Every Aut(kQ)-orbit meets the line {P_t} in finitely many points.

Consequently a dense constructible subset of V_A consists of folds whose behaviours are those of the line, each
behaviour occurring at finitely many t. **Generically, the behaviours with image A form exactly a one-parameter
family.**

**Proof.**
1. **Irreducibility.**
   - Singular (`minAssGTZ` over ℚ) finds one minimal associated prime, of dimension 7 (v2.45). So V_A is irreducible
     over ℚ.
   - P₂ = P₀ + 2N is a ℚ-rational point with dim T_{P₂}V_A = 7, computed exactly. So it is smooth and lies on a
     unique geometric component.
   - That component is Galois-stable, and Galois permutes the geometric components of a ℚ-irreducible variety
     transitively. So there is only one: V_A is geometrically irreducible.
2. **Φ lands in V_A.**
   - Δ-compatible automorphisms map folds to folds (`transport`, Lean).
   - A class weighting φ_λ maps P_t to a fold whenever P_t is compatible with Δ_λ = Σ λ_c Δ_c (v2.23 Lemma).
     Compatibility is linear in λ. Exactly over ℚ[t], the admissible weights along the whole line form
     W = span{(0, 1, 0), (1, 0, 1)}, which contains every λ = (s, r, s).
   - The weightings normalise G and the shear, so H′ = Aut_Δ·T_W is a group whose elements map each P_t to a fold.
     Elements of H′_A also fix A, so Φ lands in V_A.
3. **Φ is dominant.**
   - In characteristic 0, Lie(H′_A) = 𝔥′ = {D ∈ Der_Δ ⊕ Lie(T_W) : D(A) ⊆ A}, which has dimension 6 exactly.
   - The differential of Φ at (1, t₀) is (D, τ) ↦ [D, P_{t₀}] + τN. At t₀ = 2 its rank is 7, exactly over ℚ.
   - So the closure of the image has dimension 7 = dim V_A, and Φ is dominant by irreducibility.
4. **Finite intersection.**
   - Exactly over ℚ, N ∉ [Der(kQ), P₀] and N ∉ [Der(kQ), P₂]. The rank condition is Zariski-open in t, so
     N ∉ [Der, P_t] for all but finitely many t.
   - Suppose an Aut(kQ)-orbit O met the line in infinitely many points. O is locally closed, so O ∩ line is open
     dense in the line. At a generic t the tangent N would then lie in T_{P_t}O = [Der, P_t], a contradiction.
   - (Aut(kQ) is an algebraic group with Lie algebra Der(kQ) in characteristic 0.)
5. **Consequence.**
   - The image of Φ contains a dense constructible U ⊆ V_A (Chevalley). Every point of U is h·P_t·h⁻¹ with h an
     algebra automorphism, so its behaviour is that of P_t.
   - By step 4 each behaviour occurs at finitely many t. ∎

**What is used:**
- `line_isFold` (Lean, v2.44) and `transport` (Lean, v2.34);
- the v2.23 class-weighting lemma (on paper);
- exact ℚ computations in `analysis/v2_45_singular_VA.py`, `analysis/v2_46_slice.py` and
  `analysis/v2_46_exact_checks.py`.

The rank and dimension statements are exact rational computations, not finite-field observations.

## 3. Task 3: dominant slice

**Result:** proved; §0, steps 2–3. The obligation the brief stressed, that the conjugates remain folds, is met
without integrating any tangent vectors: every element of H′ maps the folds on the line to folds, by the two lemmas
cited.

Two pre-registered predictions failed on the way, and both were informative:
- **21:** P_t is not compatible with Δ_a or Δ_c separately; only (1, 0, 1)-type weightings act.
- **22:** t = 1 is a special point of the line, where the rank is 5 + 1.
