## 1. The general census

`gcensus.py` generalizes v2.21's kernel fibration to any acyclic quiver with one parallel pair:
1. K = ker Δ is computed mod p, and the algebra folds of K are enumerated.
2. Complement paths are processed in length order. Each P(x) is the unique Δ-preimage of (P ⊗ P)Δx plus a K-component, enumerated one column at a time with progressively filtered masked checks.
3. Fibres are computed for one kernel fold per torus orbit, since G and the grading act on K only by δ ↦ λδ. The others are generated by the grading.
4. Orbits of G = {column-sum-1 substitutions of the pair} are counted, with or without the grading torus.

It reproduces v2.18 (K₂: 64 and 164 folds, 20 orbits) and v2.21 (1⇉2→3: 219 folds, 71 orbits, the same fibre histogram) **[X1]**.

**Symmetries [X3].** I solved exactly for derivations D with ΔD − (D ⊗ 1 + 1 ⊗ D)Δ = c·Δ. These are the infinitesimal symmetries of the fold equations; c ≠ 0 allows a rescaling of Δ. For K₂, 1⇉2→3 and 1⇉2, 1→3 the solution space is **Lie(G) ⊕ k·E**, of dimension 3, where E is the path-length grading (c = 1). So Aut_Δ° = G ≅ Aff(1), and G × 𝔾_m is the whole connected symmetry group. Nothing hidden enlarges it.

## 3. The families

At 𝔽₅ the four free (G × 𝔾_m)-orbits of 1⇉2, 1→3 have representatives such as (δ = α₁ − α₂, γ: 1 → 3)

  P(e₁) = 0, P(e₂) = −e₃, P(e₃) = e₃, P(α₁) = 0, P(α₂) = P(γ) = e₁ − δ.

Conjugating by the grading at λ = 1/t gives P_t with P_t(γ) = t·e₁ − δ. Over ℚ(t) **[X5]**:
- P_t is a Δ-compatible fold for every t ≠ 0.
- τ(P) = vertex part of P(γ) is **G-invariant**: G fixes the vertices and γ and sends arrows to arrows, so (gPg⁻¹)(γ) = g(P(γ)) has the same vertex part.
- τ(P_t) = t·e₁ (or t·(e₂ + e₃) in one family).

So t is a genuine Aut_Δ-modulus, and the grading rescales it away. The mechanism is the one in v2.17's cyc A: an arrow is sent to a **vertex constant** plus a parallel difference. Δ(P(γ)) = (P ⊗ P)(e₁ ⊗ e₃) = 0 because P(e₁) = 0, and Δ kills both the constant and δ. That requires γ to share an endpoint with the pair in the same orientation. Otherwise the vertex term cannot be absorbed into the kernel this way.

**Gloss.** *When an instant sends out twin steps and one more step besides, an order–chaos split can lean on the instant itself by any nonzero amount. No relabelling of the twins detects the difference; only a change of the clock's scale does.*

## 5. Open questions

1. **Prove the fork rule**, and give the number of families: 4 for one same-orientation arrow, 26 for one at each end.
2. **Conjecture 3″ beyond one pair**: K₃ plus an arrow, two parallel pairs, and pairs on cycles.
3. A proof of Conjecture 3‴ (v2.21) for 1⇉2→3, now as the model case of the "no fork" regime.
4. Carried: the combinatorial criterion for unobstructed violations; Kronecker plus a loop; zigzag; dihedral; ρ.
