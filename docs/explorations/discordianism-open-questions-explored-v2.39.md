## 1. The argument

If Φ·P = P′·Φ, compare row v (a vertex) in column c. On the left, Φ's row v is the unit row, so the entry is P v c.
On the right it is Σ_l P′ v l · Φ l c. Suppose P′'s row v vanishes in all columns of length ≥ len c. Then every term
vanishes: either len l < len c, and Φ l c = 0, or len l ≥ len c, and P′ v l = 0. So P v c = 0 (`row_zero`).

For a pair i ≠ j, choose a vertex where the depth vectors (v2.38 §3) differ. In the deeper row, pick an entry at full
depth that is ±1; on F1 and F3 it is ±t, hence the hypothesis t ≠ 0. Intertwining forces that entry to be 0, which is
a contradiction. Use Φ when family i is deeper. Otherwise use Ψ with Ψ·P^{(j)} = P^{(i)}·Ψ, which follows from the
hypotheses.
