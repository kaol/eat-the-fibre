# Discordianism: Open Questions Explored (v2.68): Conjecture 3ᶜ for 1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3, in every characteristic

*v2.67 proved 1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3 finite in characteristic 0. One of its 21 vertex blocks (100,001,001) needed the new
orbit stratification, and the v2.58 argument for "uniform in q" does not apply to those certificates. This round
supplies the missing argument. It does not use the group's Lie algebra beyond first derivatives, so it holds in
every characteristic.
- **Lemma C ("contained orbits", characteristic-free).** It counts the orbits contained in a closed set. It needs no
  invariance of that set and no Seidenberg-type theorem, so it holds in characteristic p.
- **Specialisation of the orbit tree to all p ∉ {2, 3}.** For every node of the 72-node tree, the closure ideal,
  the radical membership of the children's intersection, the weighting compatibility and the root coverage were
  computed over ℤ. Only 2 and 3 appear as candidate bad primes.
- **p = 2 and p = 3 directly.** The orbit stratification was redone in characteristic p. Both give finitely many
  classes: 326 nodes at p = 2, 72 nodes at p = 3.
- **Theorem.** Conjecture 3ᶜ holds for 1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3: the 𝔽_q census count is at most 10^85.3 for every prime power q
  (the census gives 752 at q = 3 and 5).*

## 1. Lemma C: contained orbits (any characteristic)

**Setting.** Let k be an algebraically closed field of any characteristic. Let S be the vertex-block stratum over k,
and H the connected smooth group Der_Δ-group × T_W. Here W is the lattice of class weightings compatible on all of S.
- Every element of H acts on S by a census move. Compatibility is what makes the weighting moves census moves (v2.23).
- ξ̃_a are integral vector fields: [D̃_a, P] in the stratum coordinates, with D̃_a an integral basis of Lie(H).
- Each ξ̃_a is the tangent at t = 0 of t ↦ γ_a(t)·P for a curve γ_a in H with γ_a(0) = 1. This holds because H is
  smooth.

**Lemma C.** Let Z ⊆ S be closed with dim Z ≤ e, and let f be an e × e minor of the integral orbit matrix. Let J be
any ideal generated by:
- I(Z) and f;
- elements of the form ξ̃_a(h) − Σ c_i g_i, with h, g_i already in J and integer (or 𝔽_p) coefficients c_i.

Then every H-orbit O ⊆ Z of dimension below e lies in V(J). The orbits contained in Z of dimension e number at most
the number of e-dimensional components of Z.

*Proof.*
- **The top orbits.** An orbit of dimension e contained in Z is dense in a component of Z, and two such orbits in
  one component meet.
- **Lower orbits lie in V(f).** At each point of an orbit O of dimension below e, the rank of the orbit matrix is at
  most dim O < e, in any characteristic: the matrix's row space is the image of Lie(H), which lies in the tangent
  space of O. So f vanishes on O.
- **The generators of J vanish on O.** Induct on the generators. If h vanishes on the H-stable set O, then
  h(γ_a(t)x) ≡ 0 for x ∈ O, and its derivative at t = 0 is (ξ̃_a h)(x) = 0. ∎

**The recursion.** By Lemma C, every orbit contained in Z either is one of the top ones, or lies in V(J). Being
irreducible, it then lies in one irreducible component of V(J). So:
- if every node of a tree of closed sets admits such a J whose components form the next level, of lower dimension,
- and the roots cover S,

then S contains at most Σ_nodes (number of top-dimensional components) H-orbits. That is at most the sum of the
degrees.

**Why this is enough.** No node needs to be H-stable. H-stability is what fails in characteristic p, since a
ξ-stable ideal need not be group-stable there. The group is fixed at the root level. For 1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3, block
100,001,001, every node of the v2.67 tree already had the root's weightings, so the char 0 tree is a fixed-group
tree. A rerun with `--fixed` gives the same 72 nodes.

- **No failures elsewhere.** No C1 leading coefficient other than ±1, no failed dimension, minor or weighting.
- **The coverage C5 is exact over ℤ:** all 148 generators of K_roots lie in the stratum ideal, with no candidate

- **The candidates come from C3,** through the Rabinowitsch integers. Their prime factors are only 2 and 3:

The whole run takes 11 minutes. The root node is the slowest: 90 s, mostly the 21 Rabinowitsch bases.

## 3. p = 2 and p = 3 directly (`analysis/v2_68_charp.py`)

The same recursion, computed in characteristic p from scratch:
1. **Roots:** the minimal primes of the stratum ideal over 𝔽_p.
2. **Weightings:** compatibility of the weightings checked on each root.
3. **Minors:** for each node, an e × e minor outside I_Z, found first by trying the char 0 pivot positions, then
   by fraction-free elimination modulo the prime (the generic point), then at closed points of random linear
   sections.
4. **Children:** the closure over 𝔽_p, then its minimal primes.

## 4. The theorem

**Theorem.** For every prime power q, the number of local-torus classes of Folds(𝔽_q(1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3)) is at most
10^85.3.

*Proof.* The bound has three parts:
- **Vertex blocks:** they are the 21 reductions of the char 0 points in every characteristic (unit leading
  coefficients, v2.66).
- **The other 20 blocks:** v2.58-type certificates (v2.66): 3640 specialisation claims, every candidate prime
  checked. Their Bézout count is 10^75.7 (`bound_triple_20blocks.txt`).
- **Block 100,001,001:** at most 2663 geometric orbits for every p, by §§2–3.

From geometric orbits to 𝔽_q classes, the argument is that of v2.58 (Lang). Each geometric orbit splits into at most
the order of its stabiliser's component group. For this quiver the stabiliser factor is the same one the v2.66 driver uses: 10^9.6, from 16
coordinates of the census group and paths of length ≤ 2. Total:
10^75.7 · 10^9.6 ≈ 10^85.3. ∎

The bound is far from the truth (752), as in v2.58. Its role is to be independent of q.

**Status of Conjecture 3ᶜ.** It is now proved, uniformly in q and in every characteristic, for 17 quivers. Counting
both members of each dual pair:
- the 13 of v2.66;
- Q₀, Q₁, Q₂ (v2.58, v2.59, v2.65);
- 1⇛2⇇3 + 1→3;
- with duality, all their opposites.

These are all the unobstructed quivers of the census list as it stood before v2.66. The multiplicity variants
added in v2.66 (K₄, 1⇛2→3→4, …) are covered only where duality applies (1→2⇛3, 1⇛2 + 3→2).
